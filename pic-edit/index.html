● companion app for Lazy Pro Trip

The car, cut out, in exactly the right frame.

LPT Pic Edit does one job and does it properly: it turns an ordinary photo of a car into the hero image the Lazy Pro Trip dashboard was designed for — cut out from its background, lit with a glow, and exported at exactly 1560 × 720. That is the 13 : 6 frame Lazy Pro Trip expects, so the picture drops in without cropping and without guessing about margins.

01 — how it goes

Three taps to a picture that fits

No layers, no masking, nothing to learn.

1

Import a photo

From the library or straight from the camera. Keep the whole car in the frame — the app takes care of the rest.

2

Cut out and light it

Remove the background in one tap, then tap any individual objects you want to keep. The glow follows the outline of the car.

3

Export

A PNG is written to your gallery in the 13 : 6 frame. With a transparent background, the transparency survives the import into Lazy Pro Trip.

02 — tools

Five tabs, one decision each

Cutout and glow

Subject detection runs through Apple's Vision framework, on the device. The glow does not trace the picture frame — it traces the outline of the car itself.

  • Background removal in one tap
  • Tap an individual object to keep it
  • Nine glow colours, among them LPT Blue, White and Gold
  • Intensity and spread on their own sliders

A frame that matches the app

A photo of a car is nearly always narrower than 13 : 6, which is why the Fill button matters more here than all the filters together — without it the whole picture would occupy barely half the width.

  • Five ratios: LPT 13 : 6, 1 : 1, 4 : 3, 16 : 9, 3 : 2
  • Zoom, tilt ±90°, lateral and vertical offset
  • Padding around the object from 0 to 30 %
  • Background: LPT Dark, White or Graphite, with adjustable opacity
Retouching

Number plates and faces

A round soft brush blurs a number plate or a face in the background. The stroke sticks to the object, so zoom, tilt and canvas changes do not move the blur.

Privacy

No photo leaves the phone

Everything — subject detection, rendering and export — runs on the device. No account, no server, no upload.

Main app

Lazy Pro Trip

A business mileage log: you confirm the start, the app records the route, the stops and the distance. The picture you make here ends up on its dashboard.
