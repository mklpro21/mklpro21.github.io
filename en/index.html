Mileage that writes itself while you drive.

You decide when a trip begins and ends. Everything between those two taps is recorded for you: the distance, the route on the map, the stops and how long each one lasted. By the time you have to report it, the log is already written.

01 — how a trip goes

Three steps, two of them yours

The app does not decide for you. It only decides when to write something down.

1

You get in the car

When your phone connects over Bluetooth or CarPlay, the app asks whether a trip is beginning. One tap is enough — or a photo of the odometer if you want it exact.

2

You drive

From there nothing is required of you. The route, the distance and the stops are recorded in the background, even with the screen off and the phone in a pocket.

3

You end the trip

Mark it business or private, add a client and a purpose — or leave that to the automatic export at the end of the period.

02 — what gets recorded

The data you would otherwise collect by hand

Every stop, found on its own

Stops are detected without you touching anything. The app records where you stopped, when, and for how long — then waits for you to say whether it was business or private.

  • The route is drawn on the map
  • The address of a stop is looked up automatically
  • Dictate the purpose of a stop instead of typing it
  • Tracking continues in the background, screen off

Distance that matches the odometer

Satellite measurement always cuts corners a little. So you align it to your own car — once, and from then on the numbers agree.

  • Calibration against your vehicle's odometer
  • Read the odometer with a photo, no retyping
  • Several vehicles, each with its own odometer and history
  • Costs tied to a vehicle

Business and private kept apart

Every trip is marked business, private or mixed. What belongs in the claim does not get mixed with what does not.

  • Clients and trip purposes
  • Statistics by period and by vehicle
  • A history you can review and correct
03 — while you drive

In view, without the phone in your hand

You can see that it is running — and that is all you need to know while driving.

Live Activity on the Lock Screen

Home Screen widget — start and end without opening the app

CarPlay — distance, time and stops on the car display

Works with your car, or without it

Bluetooth and CarPlay are recognised, so the question arrives the moment you get in. Without a car connection the app works just as well — only the timing around the start and the end is less precise.

04 — export

The log is not for your phone, it is for accounting

So it leaves in a shape they expect — on a schedule you set once.

  • CSV and PDF, business and private separated, grouped by vehicle
  • Automatic export of completed periods — daily, weekly or monthly
  • The report can go straight to your accountant's e-mail address
  • Where local rules require a formal mileage log, the export is laid out for it — in Slovenia for FURS reporting

05 — make it yours

It speaks your language and looks the way you want

Two settings you choose once and then stop thinking about.

Seven languages

English, Slovenian, German, Spanish, Croatian, Serbian and Russian — the whole app, from the buttons to the exported report. In each of them you can also dictate the purpose of a stop instead of typing it.

Three backgrounds

Carbon, leather or light. The change applies immediately across the entire app — including the car display and the widget.

Background

Your data stays yours

Trips, vehicles and clients are stored on your device. Cloud sync is optional and starts only once you sign in — if you do not sign in, your data never leaves the phone.
