Lazy Pro Trip — aplikacija za beleženje službenih poti in kilometrine

Zadnja sprememba: 13. avgust 2026

English version below ↓ · Podpora

Na kratko

Vaše poti, vozila in stroški so shranjeni na vaši napravi. Aplikacija nima oglasov, ne uporablja analitičnih orodij in vaših podatkov ne prodaja nikomur. Prenos v oblak se zgodi samo, če se prijavite — če se ne, podatki naprave ne zapustijo.

Kateri podatki nastanejo

  • Lokacija (natančna, tudi v ozadju) — merjenje prevožene poti in zaznavanje postankov. To je osnovna funkcija aplikacije; brez nje kilometrine ni mogoče izmeriti.
  • Podatki o poteh — čas začetka in konca, prevoženi kilometri, postanki in njihovo trajanje, namen poti, oznaka službeno/zasebno.
  • Vozila — ime, model, registrska oznaka, stanje števca.
  • Stroški in stranke, če jih vnesete.
  • Fotografije števca in računov, če jih posnamete.

Kje se podatki obdelujejo

Na napravi

  • Vsi zgoraj našteti podatki so shranjeni lokalno.
  • Branje stanja števca s fotografije poteka izključno na napravi (ML Kit). Fotografija se ne pošlje nikamor.

Zunanje storitve

  • Naslovi postankov. Za pretvorbo koordinat v naslov se koordinate postanka pošljejo storitvi za geokodiranje (Apple oziroma Google, odvisno od naprave in nastavitve). Poslane so koordinate, ne vaša identiteta.
  • Zemljevid. Prikaz zemljevida naloži podatke pri ponudniku zemljevidov.
  • Diktiranje. Namen postanka lahko narekujete. Prepoznavo govora opravi sistemska storitev naprave. Če izbrani jezik na napravi ni nameščen za delovanje brez povezave, posnetek obdela strežnik ponudnika sistemske storitve (na iPhonu Apple). Aplikacija posnetkov ne hrani.
  • Sinhronizacija v oblak — izbirna. Vklopi se šele s prijavo z Apple ID. Takrat se poti, vozila, stroški in nastavitve prenesejo v bazo Supabase v Frankfurtu (eu-central-1, Evropska unija). Podatki so vezani na vaš uporabniški račun in dostopni samo vam.
  • Prijava z Apple ID. Prejmemo identifikator uporabnika in — če tako izberete — e-naslov. Apple vam omogoča skrivanje e-naslova; v tem primeru prejmemo posredovalni naslov.

Diagnostika

Aplikacija vodi tehnični dnevnik sledenja (kakovost signala, prekinitve, poraba). Dnevnik ostane na napravi in ga ne pošiljamo sami. Če nam ga ob prijavi napake pošljete vi, vsebuje tudi časovne in lokacijske podatke te poti — uporabimo ga izključno za odpravo napake in po tem izbrišemo.

Obveščanje

E-naslova ne uvrstimo na noben seznam obveščanja samodejno. Novice o aplikaciji vam pošljemo samo, če v to izrecno privolite; privolitev lahko kadar koli prekličete, in tudi če jo prekličete, aplikacija deluje enako.

Česa ne počnemo

  • Ne prikazujemo oglasov in ne uporabljamo oglaševalskih identifikatorjev.
  • Ne vgrajujemo analitičnih ali sledilnih orodij tretjih oseb.
  • Podatkov ne prodajamo in jih ne oddajamo tretjim osebam.
  • Vaših poti ne spremljamo. Do sinhroniziranih podatkov dostopamo samo za podporo — ko nas za pomoč zaprosite sami.

Kako dolgo se hranijo

Lokalni podatki ostanejo, dokler jih ne izbrišete v aplikaciji ali ne odstranite aplikacije. Sinhronizirane kopije ostanejo, dokler ne zahtevate izbrisa računa.

Vaše pravice

Kot posameznik v EU imate pravico do dostopa, popravka, izbrisa in prenosa svojih podatkov ter do ugovora obdelavi. Podatke lahko kadar koli izvozite iz aplikacije (CSV, PDF). Za izbris sinhroniziranih podatkov ali računa nam pišite na spodnji naslov; zahtevo obravnavamo najkasneje v 30 dneh.

Otroci

Aplikacija ni namenjena otrokom in od njih zavestno ne zbira podatkov.

Spremembe

Ob vsebinski spremembi tega pravilnika bo na tej strani objavljen nov datum. Priporočamo občasen ogled.

Privacy Policy

Lazy Pro Trip — business mileage and trip logging

Last updated: 13 August 2026

In short

Your trips, vehicles and costs are stored on your device. The app shows no ads, uses no analytics tools and sells your data to no one. Data is uploaded only if you sign in — if you do not, it never leaves the phone.

What data exists

  • Location (precise, including in the background) — to measure the route driven and detect stops. This is the core function of the app; without it mileage cannot be measured.
  • Trip data — start and end time, distance, stops and their duration, trip purpose, business/private marking.
  • Vehicles — name, model, registration plate, odometer reading.
  • Costs and clients, if you enter them.
  • Photographs of the odometer and receipts, if you take them.

Where data is processed

On your device

  • Everything listed above is stored locally.
  • Reading the odometer from a photo happens entirely on the device (ML Kit). The photo is not sent anywhere.

External services

  • Stop addresses. To turn coordinates into an address, the coordinates of a stop are sent to a geocoding service (Apple or Google, depending on device and settings). Coordinates are sent, not your identity.
  • Maps. Displaying the map loads data from the map provider.
  • Dictation. You may dictate the purpose of a stop. Speech recognition is performed by the device's system service. If the chosen language is not installed for offline use, the recording is processed on the system provider's servers (on iPhone, Apple's). The app does not keep recordings.
  • Cloud sync — optional. It starts only once you sign in with Apple. Trips, vehicles, costs and settings are then stored in a Supabase database in Frankfurt (eu-central-1, European Union). The data is tied to your account and accessible only to you.
  • Sign in with Apple. We receive a user identifier and, if you choose, an email address. Apple lets you hide your address; in that case we receive a relay address.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a technical tracking log (signal quality, interruptions, battery use). The log stays on the device and we do not collect it ourselves. If you send it to us with a bug report, it also contains times and locations from that trip — we use it solely to fix the fault and delete it afterwards.

Messages from us

We do not add your address to any mailing list automatically. We send news about the app only if you explicitly opt in; you may withdraw consent at any time, and the app works exactly the same if you do.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell your data and do not pass it to third parties.
  • We do not monitor your trips. We access synchronised data for support only — when you ask us for help.

Retention

Local data stays until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Synchronised copies stay until you request account deletion.

Your rights

If you are in the EU you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, and to object to processing. You can export your data from the app at any time (CSV, PDF). To delete synchronised data or your account, write to the address below; requests are handled within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, a new date will appear on this page.
